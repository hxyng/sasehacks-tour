How do I register?

It’s free and takes two minutes. Sign up as a team of 3 to 5, or solo and we’ll match you. Confirm with the email we send a week before; if you don’t, your spot goes to the waitlist. Link coming soon: follow @ou.sase.

Who can join? Do I need to code?

Any OU student, any major, no SASE membership needed. No experience needed either: mentors visit every table, and teams need designers and presenters too.

When is it, and is there food?

Date announced soon, 8:00 AM to 7:15 PM. Not overnight. Lunch and snacks are provided.

What’s the prompt?

The theme is SASEHack for Good: build something for your community. The exact prompt is revealed at the opening ceremony.

How is it judged? Are there prizes?

Expo style: judges visit your table for about six minutes. Prizes go to the top teams; details soon.

What should I bring?

A laptop, charger, student ID and a water bottle.

Where is it?

OU’s Norman campus; the building is being confirmed. Need accommodations? Email sase@ou.edu.
